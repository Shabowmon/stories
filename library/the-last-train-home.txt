Title: The Last Train Home
Hero: Daniel Vale, 28
Genre: Young Adult · Mystery · Supernatural
Colors: #263238, #d4a574
Blurb: A stranded passenger boards a train that shouldn't exist and discovers it carries people to the one place they have been afraid to leave behind.

Episode 1: Platform 6

The station was supposed to close at midnight.

Daniel checked the clock.

11:47 PM.

He was alone on Platform 6.

At least, he thought he was.

Rain tapped against the glass roof above him. Somewhere in the distance, a vending machine hummed.

Daniel looked down the empty tracks.

No train.

He checked his phone.

No signal.

"Great," he muttered.

Then the station speakers crackled.

"Attention passengers. The 11:55 service to Northbridge is now arriving on Platform 6."

Daniel looked up.

He had never heard of Northbridge.

The station sign above him clearly said Bellhaven.

Two headlights appeared in the distance.

A train emerged from the darkness.

It was old. Much older than the trains that normally stopped here.

The doors opened.

There was nobody inside.

Then Daniel's phone vibrated.

One new message.

He stared at the screen.

The message was from his own phone number.

DON'T GET ON THE TRAIN.

Daniel slowly looked back toward the tracks.

The train doors were still open.

Then someone inside the empty train whispered his name.

"Daniel."

He froze.

The doors began to close.

Episode 2: Northbridge

Daniel stepped onto the train.

He wasn't sure why.

Maybe curiosity.

Maybe fear.

Maybe because the voice had sounded exactly like his father's.

His father had been dead for eight years.

The doors shut behind him.

The train began moving.

Daniel walked through the cars.

Every seat was empty.

Every window showed darkness.

Then he noticed something strange.

The digital display above the door read:

NORTHBRIDGE — 12:14 AM

He checked his phone.

12:01 AM.

The train was displaying a time that hadn't happened yet.

Daniel sat down.

Across from him was an old woman.

He hadn't seen her when he entered.

She was staring directly at him.

"You shouldn't be here," she said.

Daniel swallowed.

"Where is Northbridge?"

The woman smiled sadly.

"You're going to find out."

"How?"

She pointed toward the window.

Daniel looked.

The darkness outside had disappeared.

Instead, he saw a suburban street.

His street.

His house.

His wife standing on the front porch.

Daniel jumped to his feet.

"Stop the train."

The woman shook her head.

"You can't stop it."

Daniel stared through the glass.

His wife suddenly looked toward the train.

As if she could see him.

Then she raised her hand.

And waved goodbye.

Episode 3: The Passenger

Daniel turned back toward the woman.

"Who are you?"

She didn't answer.

"How do you know me?"

Still nothing.

Daniel moved closer.

Then he noticed the photograph in her hands.

It showed a group of people standing beside the same train.

Daniel recognized one of them.

His father.

Daniel grabbed the photograph.

"Where did you get this?"

The woman finally looked up.

"Your father took that picture."

"That's impossible."

"He rode this train too."

Daniel stared at her.

"What happened to him?"

The woman looked toward the ceiling.

"He got off."

Daniel waited.

"And?"

"He forgot why he came."

The train suddenly shook.

The lights went out.

For several seconds, there was complete darkness.

Then a voice came over the speakers.

"Next stop."

The lights returned.

The old woman was gone.

Daniel looked around the empty carriage.

Then he noticed something written on the window.

Words scratched into the glass.

DANIEL, IF YOU SEE THIS, DON'T GET OFF.

Episode 4: The Choice

The train slowed.

A sign appeared outside the window.

NORTHBRIDGE.

The doors opened.

Daniel stood.

On the platform was his father.

Older than Daniel remembered.

Gray hair.

A tired face.

But unmistakably him.

"Dad?"

His father smiled.

"You came."

Daniel stepped toward him.

"Are you really here?"

His father nodded.

"I've been waiting."

Daniel's eyes filled with tears.

"Why didn't you come home?"

His father's expression changed.

"Because I couldn't."

"Why?"

His father looked past him.

"The train doesn't take people where they want to go."

Daniel looked back at the train.

"What does it take?"

"People who aren't ready to let go."

Daniel didn't understand.

His father stepped closer.

"You've been blaming yourself for what happened to me."

Daniel shook his head.

"No."

"You were seventeen."

"I should have been there."

"You couldn't have saved me."

Daniel looked down.

His father placed a hand on his shoulder.

"Go home."

Daniel looked at the train.

Then at his father.

"If I leave, will I ever see you again?"

His father smiled.

"You never stopped seeing me."

The train whistle sounded.

Daniel stepped backward.

The doors began to close.

His father said one final thing.

"Live your life, Daniel."

The doors shut.

The train began moving.

Episode 5: Home

Daniel woke up on a bench.

Morning sunlight filled the station.

A cleaner was sweeping the floor nearby.

Daniel sat up.

"Where's the train?"

The cleaner looked at him strangely.

"What train?"

Daniel checked his phone.

7:13 AM.

He had twelve missed calls from his wife.

And one new message.

I'M WORRIED. PLEASE CALL ME.

Daniel stared at the screen.

Then he smiled.

He called her.

She answered immediately.

"Where are you?"

"I'm at the station."

"What happened?"

Daniel looked down the tracks.

Nothing.

Just the morning sun.

"I'll tell you when I get home."

He stood.

Before leaving, he noticed something on the bench.

A photograph.

He picked it up.

It was the same photograph from the train.

His father stood among a group of passengers.

But there was something different.

Daniel was in the photograph too.

Standing beside his father.

He turned the photograph over.

Written on the back were four words.

YOU MADE IT HOME.

Daniel put the photograph in his pocket.

Then he walked toward the station exit.

For the first time in eight years, he wasn't looking back.
